# Code of conduct

Contributors must be able to disagree precisely without personal attack, harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.

Expected behaviour:

- criticise claims, designs, measurements, and code rather than people;
- state uncertainty and distinguish evidence from preference;
- do not pressure anyone to disclose private infrastructure, data, identity, or credentials;
- respect licence, attribution, and security boundaries;
- report conflicts of interest and benchmark limitations;
- accept that maintainers may reject technically impressive work that increases risk or maintenance burden.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate publication of secrets or private data, benchmark fabrication, licence concealment, and knowingly unsafe deployment advice.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or the project.
